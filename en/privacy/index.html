Anilar Privacy Policy

Русский

Anilar does not collect data about you. The app has no server of its own, no analytics and no ads.

Your photos

The app reads photos directly from the storages you connect and shows them on your device. Downscaled copies are kept only in a cache on the device. They are never sent to the developer.

Access to photos on the device

If you add "Photos on this phone", or a USB drive on a TV, the app asks for permission to read photos from the system media library. It is used only to show the pictures on that device: they are not copied anywhere except the slideshow cache and are not sent to the developer or to other devices. You can allow access to selected photos only, and revoke the permission in system settings.

Favorites and hidden photos

"Add to favorites" and "Don't show" marks are kept only on the device where you set them, and are deleted together with the source.

Sign-in data

Passwords and access tokens are kept in the system's secure storage on the device and are sent only to the server they belong to.

Sending to a TV

When pairing, the phone sends the sources and settings you choose directly to the TV over the local network, encrypted. The camera is used only to read the QR code; images are not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the app store. Purchase details are kept on the device, and the phone may send proof of purchase to a paired TV.

Problem reports

The "Send report" button opens an email with a log that contains no paths, addresses or passwords. You send it yourself and can review it first.

Deleting data

Removing a source erases its sign-in data, index and cache from the device. Uninstalling the app erases everything else.
